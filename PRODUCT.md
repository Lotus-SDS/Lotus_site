# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Businesses and startups, mostly Portuguese-speaking in Brazil, looking for a software development partner. They arrive with a project in mind (a web system, a mobile app, cloud infrastructure, or an AI integration) and use the site to judge whether Lotus is credible and capable before starting a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Lotus Soluções em Desenvolvimento de Software is a software studio founded in 2025 in Espírito Santo, Brasil. The site at lotusdev.com.br is its front door: it explains what Lotus builds, who is behind it, and turns interest into an email to contato@lotusdev.com.br. Success is a qualified visitor sending that email.

## Positioning

"Rigor técnico com sensibilidade estética": Lotus claims that powerful software does not need to be complicated, and that real sophistication is delivering intuitive, efficient solutions. It is founder-led: the two co-founders are the team a client talks to.

## Operating Context

Visitors reach the site by the domain, LinkedIn, GitHub, or referral, on desktop and mobile. They compare Lotus with other studios and agencies, then continue the conversation by email.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Four service lines: Desenvolvimento Web (complex systems, interactive dashboards, high-conversion landing pages with React and Next.js), Aplicativos Móveis (native and hybrid apps for iOS and Android), Arquitetura Cloud (scalable, secure infrastructure on AWS or Azure with DevOps and CI/CD), Integração com IA (AI models that automate processes and generate insights).
- The site is a static single-page app (React + Vite + Tailwind) served by nginx behind Traefik. There is no backend, so contact happens through `mailto:` links, not forms.
- All copy is Brazilian Portuguese.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "Lotus"; domain lotusdev.com.br; tagline "Soluções de desenvolvimento de software"; footer line "Inovação que floresce através do código. Soluções digitais para um mundo conectado."
- Logo: a lotus flower drawn with circuit-board traces, in lime/olive green (`public/lotuslogo.png`, transparent PNG 948×672; `public/LOTUSGREEN.svg` embeds the same raster). Favicons derive from it.
- Voice: confident and warm, with growth and plant metaphors (floresce, raiz, cultivando ideias, colheita) and a playful code snippet.
- User decision (2026-09-28): the redesign follows two dark reference sites the user supplied (a business-agency landing page and an AI-product landing page), with the logo's lime green as the accent instead of the references' electric blue.

## Evidence on Hand

- Founded 2025; "100% foco em qualidade" (claim already on the site); four service lines; based in Espírito Santo, Brasil.
- Leadership: João Paulo G. Azevedo (Co-Founder & CEO, github.com/jpguelerazevedo) and Pedro Henrique B. Duarte (Co-Founder & CEO, github.com/PhenBD); portraits are their GitHub avatars.
- Organization GitHub: github.com/Lotus-SDS. Email: contato@lotusdev.com.br.
- The LinkedIn link is a generic placeholder (https://linkedin.com), kept by the user until a real URL exists.
- Absent, and must not be fabricated: client names or logos, portfolio projects, testimonials, project counts, satisfaction rates, or support-hour claims.

## Product Principles

1. Prove with what is true: founding year, services, stack, and the founders themselves, never borrowed agency numbers.
2. Sophistication through simplicity: the site should feel as intuitive and efficient as the software Lotus promises.
3. Founder-led and direct: the people are part of the offer.
4. Contact is always one step away.
